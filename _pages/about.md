👨🏻‍💻 Today I work as a **business developer in deep tech**: I help turn science into products, companies and impact. At **Vaimee** (Italy) I work on the commercial strategy and the creation of a spin-off, and as a **senior business developer for the Horizon Booster Service (META)** I support EU-funded research projects in finding their value proposition, product–market fit and business plan.

🧬 Before that, I was a scientist: a **PhD in Molecular Neurobiology** in Osnabrück on Tau and microtubules, and a **postdoc in Bioinformatics and AI** at the Université libre de Bruxelles, in the lab of Fabrizio Pucci and Marianne Rooman, where I developed **SOuLMuSiC**, a tool that predicts how mutations change protein solubility.

📽️ I'm passionate about making science accessible and engaging. As communication scientist of the COST Action [Machine Learning 4 Non Globular Proteins](https://ml4ngp.eu/) I produce interviews and videos, write newsletters and look after its social channels.

Twist and Shout: The Unexpected Turns of My Path
======
My scientific career starts in South Italy, in Bari. There my passion for turtles and dolphins (characteristic of my city, Taranto) led me to study Biological Sciences.
In one of the last exams, I discovered the critical role of mitochondria in brain health and disease, particularly how they are affected in conditions like Parkinson’s Disease. This moment reshaped my scientific pursuits, leading me deeper into molecular neurobiology and neuroscience.

🧠 **Trieste** — the International Master in Neuroscience, completed in 2019 with 110/110 cum laude.

🌍 **Lisbon and Zurich** — the next stops of the journey, before the PhD.

🔬 **Osnabrück and Milan** — my PhD in Molecular Neurobiology (2020–2023): how extracellular Tau affects Tau and microtubules inside neurons, with a collaboration in Milan on how synuclein interacts with tubulin. Along the way I taught practical courses and supervised four theses.

💻 **Brussels** — from the bench to the computer: a postdoc applying machine learning to proteins, from solubility prediction to AI-based structure prediction.

🚀 **From science to business** — since 2025 I have been bringing all of this to the other side of research: helping projects and start-ups go from results to the market.
